Need a little extra support?

If you're feeling very overwhelmed or finding things difficult to manage, consider talking to a trusted adult, parent or guardian, teacher, counselor, or another professional you trust. You don't have to figure everything out alone.

1A parent, guardian, or relative you feel safe with.
2A teacher, school counselor, or campus wellness center.
3A doctor, therapist, or other licensed mental health professional.

Reaching out is a sign of strength, not weakness. You deserve support from real people who can help. 🌿
